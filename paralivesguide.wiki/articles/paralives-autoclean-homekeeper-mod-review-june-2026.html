AutoClean Homekeeper Mod Review: The Must-Have Auto-Cleaning Mod for Paralives

Tired of playing maid? This single mod wipes tables, mops floors, scrubs toilets, and tidies clutter — automatically, every night, while your Paras sleep. It's the #1 quality-of-life mod on the Workshop for a reason.

📅 Published: June 3, 2026·🔄 Updated: June 3, 2026

Let's be honest: the chore loop is the worst part of Paralives. Every single day you're directing your Paras to wipe counters, mop floors, scrub toilets, and pick up scattered clothes. Miss a few days and the house turns into a biohazard — triggering disgust and irritation moodlets that tank your carefully stacked Ecstatic buffs. The AutoClean Homekeeper mod solves all of this in one click.

⚡ Three Core Features

🧹 1. Fully Automated Whole-House Cleaning

Every night while your Paras are asleep, the mod silently sweeps through the entire house: floor stains, kitchen grease, bathroom grime, scattered trash — all cleaned without a single manual command. You wake up to a spotless home every morning.

⚙️ 2. Customizable Zone Toggle

The mod includes its own settings panel. You can selectively disable outdoor/yard cleaning while keeping indoor auto-clean active — perfect for players with custom gardens who want to manage landscaping manually.

📦 3. Automatic Tidying & Decluttering

Books left on the floor, clothes draped over chairs, toys scattered in the hallway — the mod automatically returns every misplaced item to its proper storage. This dramatically reduces the "Cluttered Home" negative moodlet that so many players struggle with.

📥 How to Install

  1. 🛒 Open the Steam Workshop, search for "AutoClean Homekeeper", and hit Subscribe.
  2. 🎮 Launch Paralives. On the main menu, open the Mods list and make sure the mod is checked as enabled.
  3. 💾 Load your save — the mod activates immediately. No configuration needed (but you can tweak the settings panel if you want).

⚠️ Critical Warnings — Read Before Installing

  • 🚫 Never run this alongside other housekeeping or auto-cleaning mods. Functional conflicts can cause furniture to vanish or floor textures to glitch out.
  • 🗑️ If you ever uninstall: manually clean the entire house before removing the mod. If you uninstall while there's accumulated filth, it all loads in at once and your house becomes an instant disaster zone.
💡 Verdict: This is the first mod I install on every new save. It reclaims hours of gameplay that would otherwise be spent on chores, freeing you up to focus on building, socializing, and career progression. For a free Workshop mod, the quality is outstanding.
