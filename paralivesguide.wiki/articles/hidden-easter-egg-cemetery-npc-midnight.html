Hidden Easter Egg Found: Mysterious NPC Appears at Cemetery Midnight

Reddit user u/ParaExplorer discovered a cloaked figure named Ashera who only appears at midnight in the cemetery. Here's how to trigger the encounter and claim the rare reward.

📅 Published: June 1, 2026·🔄 Updated: June 1, 2026
🕛 Trigger Time: 12:00 AM in‑game. Arrive by 11:30 PM and wait by the large tombstone with strange symbols.

🪦 How to Trigger the Encounter

  1. Travel to the cemetery in the northwest corner of town around 11:30 PM.
  2. Stand next to the large tombstone engraved with unusual symbols at the center.
  3. Wait until the clock strikes 12:00 AM.
  4. A female NPC named Ashera, wearing a black hooded cloak, emerges from behind the tombstone.

👤 Who Is Ashera?

Ashera speaks in an ancient language, telling stories of the "Old World" and "Sleeping Guardians." She does not appear on the map and is only present during the midnight hour.

Reward: Speak to her on three consecutive nights to build affinity. She will gift you the "Dream Stone" — a rare item that doubles all skill gain for 24 hours and prevents negative emotions.

🐱 Bonus Discovery

Return to the cemetery at 3:00 AM on the same night, and Ashera will be gone. In her place, a pure black cat appears. It follows you home, stays for a full day, and grants a "Lucky" buff to your entire household.

The community speculates this may be foreshadowing future supernatural content — vampires, witches, or other life states. For now, it's a delightful little secret tucked away in Paralives Town. Go check it out tonight!
