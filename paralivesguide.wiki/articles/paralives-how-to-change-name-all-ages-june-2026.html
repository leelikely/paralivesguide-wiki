How to Change Your Para's Name: 3 Easy Methods (All Ages)

Stuck with a name you regret? No rename button in sight? Don't restart your save — here are three proven ways to change any Para's name, from babies to elders, no mods required.

📅 Published: June 3, 2026·🔄 Updated: June 3, 2026

Plenty of players type in a random name during character creation, only to realize days later that it doesn't fit their Para's personality at all. You scour every panel, every menu — but there's no rename button anywhere. This is hands-down one of the most frustrating newbie traps in Paralives Early Access. The good news: there are three reliable ways to rename any Para, covering babies, toddlers, and adults, and none of them require installing a single mod.

🪞 Method 1: Mirror Interaction (Adults & Teens — No Cheats)

This is the recommended vanilla method — no console, no save file editing, just a mirror and a few clicks.

  1. 🏠 Send your target Para back to their home lot.
  2. 🛒 Open Build Mode, grab a full-length floor mirror from the furniture catalog, and place it on a flat indoor surface. Leave at least one tile of clear space around it so your Para doesn't get stuck.
  3. 🖱️ Click the mirror and select "Change Outfit & Personal Profile" — this instantly launches the Paramaker character editor.
  4. 📝 In the bottom-right corner of the Paramaker, look at the character info card. Next to the name, you'll see three tiny dots. Click them to expand a hidden menu where you can edit first name, last name, and custom nickname.
  5. ✅ Hit save, exit, and the new name takes effect immediately.
⚠️ Heads up: Babies and toddlers haven't unlocked the self-grooming social skill yet, so they can't interact with mirrors. Use Method 2 or 3 for them instead.

💻 Method 2: Console Command (All Ages — Fastest)

This one takes literally 10 seconds and works on every Para in the game.

  1. ⌨️ Press Ctrl + F1 to bring up the built-in developer console. No hidden settings to toggle — it just works.
  2. 🖱️ Left-click the Para you want to rename. A white outline ring appears at their feet when selected.
  3. ⌨️ Type editcharacter and hit Enter — you'll instantly jump into the Paramaker.
  4. ✏️ Change the name to whatever you like, save, and exit. Done!
💡 Pro tip: The editcharacter command gives you full Paramaker access — not just the name. You can tweak appearance, traits, and outfits while you're in there, all without any restrictions.

📁 Method 3: Edit Save File Manually (No Console)

Prefer to stay out of the console entirely? You can rename your Para directly in the save files.

  1. 🚪 Completely close Paralives before doing this — editing save files while the game is running can corrupt your data.
  2. 📂 Navigate to: C:\Users\YourUsername\AppData\LocalLow\Alex Masse\Paralives\Saves
  3. 🔍 Open the folder matching your household save, then open characters.json with Notepad.
  4. 🔎 Press Ctrl + F and search for "name". Replace the text inside the quotation marks with your new name.
  5. 💾 Save the file, relaunch Paralives, and the change is live.

✨ Bonus Tips

  • 🆕 New character creation: In the Paramaker, the three-dot menu next to the name field also lets you set a middle name and custom nickname right from the start — saves you from renaming later.
  • 🗣️ NPCs still using the old name? Take your Para for a stroll around town. Once you leave the lot and come back, the game refreshes all NPC dialogue data and they'll start using the new name.
  • 💾 Always save first! Hit F5 to quicksave before editing any save files. If something goes wrong, you can reload instantly.
