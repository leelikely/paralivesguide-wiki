How to Quit & Change Jobs in Paralives (The Right Way)

The quit button isn't where you'd expect. Here's exactly where to find it, plus the smoothest strategy to switch careers without missing a paycheck.

📅 Published: June 23, 2026·🔄 Updated: June 23, 2026

🔍 Where Is the Quit Button?

It's not on the phone or at the workplace. Open the Occupations panel (click the small backpack icon on the left side of the screen). Scroll all the way to the bottom of your current job details. There you'll see a gray “Quit Job” button. Click it, confirm, and you're officially unemployed — effective immediately.

💼 How to Find a New Job

  • 📱 Phone: click Para → Use Phone → Find Job. The most convenient method, available anywhere.
  • 💻 Computer: same job listings as the phone. Can be used at home.
  • 🏪 In‑person: visit the actual venue (café, hospital, office) and speak to the manager — some jobs let you start on the spot.

Job listings refresh daily. Each position requires a certain number of Application Points, earned from skills, work experience, and traits.

🔄 The Smart Way to Switch Careers

  • 📝 Secure an offer first: apply and interview while still employed. Only quit after you've accepted the new job. Zero income gap.
  • 🌙 Part‑time bridge: if your target career requires skills you don't yet have, take a related part‑time job in the evenings while keeping your day job. Build skills and points, then switch.
  • 🔄 Cross‑industry moves: don't apply for the highest tier immediately; start from the entry level. Your previous work experience converts to partial Application Points, so it's not a total reset.

❓ FAQ & Pitfalls

  • Can I work multiple jobs? Yes, as long as schedules don't overlap. Stick to ≤2 jobs — more than that and exhaustion kills performance.
  • Do I keep my previous rank? No. Switching industries resets you to the bottom. Intra‑industry jumps may preserve some progress, but not always.
  • What if I get fired? A temporary negative moodlet appears for a few days, then disappears. No permanent record. Just find a new job.
  • Is there a sabbatical option? No unpaid leave exists yet. If you need a break, quit and re‑apply later when you're ready.
💡 Final advice: Don't job‑hop constantly. Stay in each role long enough to earn at least two promotions — otherwise you'll spread your skills thin and slow your overall career growth. Pick a direction and specialize.
