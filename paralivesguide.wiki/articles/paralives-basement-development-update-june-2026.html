Paralives Basement Feature: Official Development Update & September Beta

In today's Discord Q&A, lead developer Alex Massé revealed basements are 65% complete — and gave us the first detailed roadmap for the feature everyone's been asking for.

📅 Published: June 1, 2026·🔄 Updated: June 1, 2026
🗓️ Key Dates: Closed beta for basements — late September 2026. Public release — Q4 2026 (first major content update).

📊 Basement Progress: 65% Complete

Alex explained that basements are far more complex than simply adding an underground floor. The team is completely rewriting the terrain system and floor collision detection to ensure basements work seamlessly with all existing build tools.

🔧 Remaining Technical Challenges

  • 💡 Underground lighting system — dynamic light levels that change with depth.
  • ⛰️ Terrain deformation impact — ensuring existing above‑ground structures aren't affected when digging below.

✨ Confirmed Basement Features

  • 🏚️ Build up to 2 basement levels.
  • 🚗 Underground garages, wine cellars, home cinemas, gyms, and storage rooms.
  • 🪟 Basement windows and light wells (yes, natural light underground!).
  • 🚪 Independent external basement entrances.
  • 💧 Humidity and temperature mechanics that affect Para mood and item durability.

As always, all updates remain entirely free — no paid DLC, ever. The team reiterated that June through September will focus exclusively on bug fixes and performance, with new content arriving from October onward.
