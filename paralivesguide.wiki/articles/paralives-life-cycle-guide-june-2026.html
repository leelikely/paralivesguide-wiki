Paralives Life Cycle Guide: Birth, Aging, Marriage & Death

From a first date to the final farewell — here's the complete journey through every life stage and major life event in Paralives.

📅 Published: June 23, 2026·🔄 Updated: June 23, 2026

💞 Romantic Progression

Stranger → Acquaintance → Friend → Good Friend → Crush → Partner → Engaged → Married.

  • Early stages: build friendship with green cards first (affinity 50+).
  • Romance: pink cards unlock after friendship. Start gently — compliments and sweet talk. Spamming flirt too early causes embarrassment.
  • Commitment: at 60+ romantic affinity, “Ask to Be Partners” appears. At max, propose. Only elopement (“Elope”) is currently available; a formal wedding ceremony will be added in a future update.

🤰 Pregnancy & Birth

  • Trying for Baby: both partners must be adults, click the bed and select “Try for Baby.” Success depends on mood and health.
  • Pregnancy lasts ~21 game days across three trimesters. In the final trimester, the Para moves slower and tires easily.
  • Labor: when contractions start, choose “Go to Hospital” or have a home birth.
  • ⛔ Pregnancy ability is not tied to gender: in Paramaker you can define who can become pregnant and who can impregnate. Same‑sex couples can have biological children. Adoption is also available at the Town Hall.

👣 Life Stages (8 Total)

StageDurationKey Features
👶 Infant0‑7 daysFully dependent — feeding, diaper changes, cuddles.
🧒 Toddler7‑14 daysLearn to walk, talk, and use the potty.
🧑 Child14‑21 daysSchool starts, basic skills, simple chores.
🧑‍🦱 Teen21‑28 daysEmotions unlock, part‑time jobs, social life expands.
🧑 Young Adult28‑50 daysPrime age — career, marriage, children.
🧔 Adult50‑70 daysSkill gain slows, energy drops slightly faster.
👴 Elder70+ daysPhysical decline, eventual natural death.

Stage lengths can be customized in the Storyteller settings. Disable aging entirely to freeze a stage.

💀 Death & Afterlife

  • Natural aging: peaceful passing at the end of the Elder stage.
  • Fire: cooking accidents or ignited fireplaces can be fatal.
  • Exhaustion: ignoring the energy bar for too long can cause death by overwork.
  • Mushroom poisoning: eating wild mushrooms carries a deadly risk.

After death, an urn is left behind — keep it at home or bury it in the cemetery. Full funeral ceremonies and ghost systems are planned for future updates.

💡 Playstyle tip: Don't rush. Set unique life goals for each Para, embrace imperfection, and let unscripted moments shape your stories.
